# Personalized Nutrition Recommendation Platform

## 1. Project Title

**Personalized Nutrition Recommendation Platform**

## 2. Problem Statement

Many people find it difficult to choose suitable food and diet plans according to their individual health conditions, dietary preferences, lifestyle, and nutritional requirements. Generic diet plans are not suitable for everyone because each person's nutritional needs are different.

The proposed **Personalized Nutrition Recommendation Platform** is a full-stack web application that collects user health information, dietary preferences, and lifestyle details. Based on the provided information, the system generates personalized nutrition and diet recommendations.

The platform allows users to create an account, securely log in, enter their health and food preferences, receive personalized diet recommendations, and track their recommended nutrition plans. The user's information and recommendations are stored in a database for future access.

The system will initially use a rule-based recommendation approach and can be enhanced with an **AI/ML-based nutrition recommendation system** in future development. AI can be used to analyze user data and provide more personalized recommendations.

## 3. Objectives

* To provide secure user registration and login.
* To collect and store user health information.
* To collect dietary preferences and restrictions.
* To generate personalized nutrition recommendations.
* To store and retrieve diet recommendations from the database.
* To allow users to track their nutrition plans.
* To develop a scalable full-stack platform.
* To integrate AI/ML-based recommendations in future versions.

## 4. Main Features

### User Authentication

* User registration
* User login
* Secure authentication using JWT
* Protected user access

### Health Profile

Users can provide:

* Age
* Gender
* Height
* Weight
* Activity level
* Food preferences
* Dietary restrictions

### Nutrition Recommendation

The system analyzes the user's health profile and preferences to generate suitable nutrition recommendations such as:

* Breakfast
* Lunch
* Snacks
* Dinner
* Recommended food items
* Approximate calorie information

### Nutrition Plan Tracking

Users can view their saved recommendations and track their nutrition plans.

## 7. Expected Outcome

The system is expected to provide users with personalized nutrition recommendations based on their health information, lifestyle, and dietary preferences. It will provide a user-friendly platform for managing health profiles and viewing nutrition plans.

The system will also provide a foundation for integrating advanced AI/ML techniques to improve recommendation accuracy in future development.

## 8. Future Enhancements

* Calorie and nutrition tracking
* Meal history

* AI nutrition chatbot

* Integration with wearable health devices

## 9. Scope

The project focuses on developing a secure and scalable personalized nutrition platform that connects the frontend, backend, and database. The initial version will demonstrate user authentication, health profile management, and personalized nutrition recommendations.

The recommendations are intended for general informational and planning purposes and are not a substitute for professional medical or dietary advice.
